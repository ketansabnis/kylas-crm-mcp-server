## The headline

The "open pipeline" is far smaller than it looks. Of 150 supposedly-qualified, active deals, **only 32 (21%) are genuinely workable now** (HOT/WARM). **Half are either mis-qualified or dead:** 27 were never properly qualified (wrong contact / BANT missing → back to pre-sales), 23 are sub-ICP or non-responsive (disqualify), and 24 are real deals **rotting on the rep** (reassign). Your open book is carrying a lot of phantom pipeline — and the model + audit notes locate it precisely.

- **Back to pre-sales (27, ~₹7.0L):** never truly qualified — the dominant cause is **the contact isn't the decision-maker or the identity is fake/mismatched** (enrichment 🔴): M3M (contact is Strategy team, not DM), Umesh Khatana / Dev Sunar / Ashish Gautam / nitish / Aman Srivastava (phone resolves to a different person or a placeholder firm), plus BANT-missing intakes (Praveen, Vamsi, Jaydev, Shaurya). **This is a pre-sales qualification failure the audit already flags** — re-qualify before Sales spends another hour.
- **Disqualify (23, ~₹3.7L):** sub-ICP 2-user asks (below the 5-seat floor), price-anchored on a cheap incumbent (Kit19, B2BBricks, Accounts24), or non-responsive for months (Raj Kumar Gupta 100+ days, Farooq 5 months phone-off). Close-lost and reclaim the demo capacity.

**The systemic read:** 33% of the "qualified" book (back-to-presales + disqualify) should never have reached sales as live pipeline, and another 16% is stalling on reps. Fix qualification at the top and hygiene in the middle and the same book converts far more.

## Top-of-funnel plan — generate more *qualified* leads & revenue (this month)

1. **Reallocate ad spend to what converts.** Scale **Google** (best 5+ mix, 9.5% win) and invest in **Organic/SEO/brand** (13.7% win, our champion). **Fix or defund Facebook** — 19% of demos, 2% win — retarget to developer/large-account lookalikes or move the budget to Google.
2. **Target developers explicitly.** Developers are **80% of revenue** and close ~2x faster, yet we run more channel partners. Build campaigns off **RERA developer lists + project-launch triggers**, not generic "real-estate CRM."
3. **Concentrate demand where we win.** Pump lead-gen into **Hyderabad & Chennai** (24–27% win, starved), **root-cause Pune** (HQ, ~0 conversion), and pursue large developers even off-territory (they close remotely — ₹19.4L proof).
4. **Gate lead quality at the source.** No demo booked without **≥5 seats, verified company, real phone (auto-reject 9999999999 / single-word names), and a named decision-maker.** This alone removes the ~33% junk we just found in the open book.
5. **Wire the conversion model into the hourly CRON.** Run `score_deal.py` next to the BANT/quality audit; write **probability + band** to a deal field. Auto-route **HOT → senior rep + onsite**, and **auto-deprioritise COLD** (on June data, COLD held 1 of 25 wins).
6. **Fix attribution.** Campaign/UTM is captured on ~1 of 253 deals — you can't optimise spend you can't measure. Make **UTM/campaign mandatory** on lead capture.
7. **Grow Organic with competitive content.** We keep losing on price/features to **Zoho, Leadrat, Buildesk, Kit19** — publish developer case studies and head-to-head comparison pages; Organic is the cheapest, highest-intent channel.
8. **Retrain pre-sales on decision-maker capture.** The #1 "back to pre-sales" cause is a junior/wrong/fake contact — use **enrichment to verify identity before booking** and insist the demo includes the owner/MD.
9. **Sales-hygiene SLA + auto-reassign.** Any HOT/WARM deal idle **>21 days** escalates/reassigns automatically — that recovers the ~₹4.5L currently rotting on reps.
10. **Stand up a referral engine.** Several wins came via references (EXP Realty, Aurum). Formalise a **referral/reference program** — it's the cheapest source of high-intent, developer-grade leads.

**Net:** work the 32 HOT/WARM now (payment-link and decision-maker closes), reassign the 24 stalled deals, send 27 back to pre-sales to re-qualify, disqualify 23 — and point marketing at developers via Google + Organic while the model gates the junk out at the top.
